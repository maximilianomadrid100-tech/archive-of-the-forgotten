WHY DO THEY AGE DIFFERENTLY?

On the Moon, there is no rain or atmospheric wind to quickly erase footprints and tracks. However, radiation, micrometeorites, and extreme temperatures still affect materials.

On Mars, dust, wind, and temperature changes gradually alter the appearance of spacecraft and landing sites.
